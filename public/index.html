🐾 About Us

Welcome to our Pet Adoption & Rescue Portal!

Our mission is to help abandoned, injured, and homeless animals find loving homes and receive the care they deserve.

Through our platform, users can adopt pets, report animals in need of rescue, and track the progress of rescue requests.

Together, we can create a safer and happier world for animals. ❤️
